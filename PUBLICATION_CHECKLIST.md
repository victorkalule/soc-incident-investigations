# Ongoing Publication & Data-Handling Notes

**Repository status:** Public. This repository contains original, recruiter-facing summaries of simulated cybersecurity training work.

This page documents precautions for **future changes or evidence uploads**. It is not a declaration that a third party has approved publication. TripleTen's exact permission rules for sharing original assignment materials or screenshots have **not been independently verified**.

## Before adding more files or screenshots

- [ ] Confirm that each new artifact may be shared publicly under the relevant course, dataset, and ownership terms.
- [ ] Do not upload original training reports, proprietary instructions, confidential material, raw datasets, malware, credentials, or other restricted files.
- [ ] Remove private IP addresses, user or account details, tokens, personal information, and unapproved indicators from any evidence.
- [ ] Keep simulated training and proposed designs clearly distinguished from deployed controls and actual production incidents.
- [ ] Double-check claims, technical details, and screenshots for accuracy before publishing.

**Project-specific caution:** The original training investigations contain historical dataset identifiers and account information. Do not upload raw logs, usernames, passwords, private indicators, malware samples, or screenshots containing those fields. Clearly label incidents as training investigations rather than live incident response.

## Portfolio approach

The repository's current case studies and summaries are written in my own words. Additional technical evidence can be added when sharing rights and appropriate redaction are confirmed.
